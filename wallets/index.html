    About the results

    • Bought means tokens came from a contract such as a trading pool or launchpad. Airdrops from a contract count too.
    • Bundle means one transaction gave tokens to several wallets at once, often a sign they belong to one person (or an airdrop).
    • Contract wallets are usually smart wallets, sometimes pools.
    • A wallet is not a person: one trader can use many wallets. Early windows are often full of bots.
    • ⚠ Hidden characters in a token name and look-alike addresses (same start and end as another) are common scams. Fake tokens can even make it look as if a wallet sent them.
