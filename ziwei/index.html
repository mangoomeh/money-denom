          About Zi Wei Dou Shu
          • Zi Wei Dou Shu (“Purple Star Astrology”) places 14 main stars and many minor ones in 12 palaces, each a part of life, from the lunar date and two-hour period of birth. It's a separate system from BaZi: BaZi reads balance and timing, Zi Wei the detail of each life area.
          • The birth time is converted with the birthplace's time zone as it was then, with optional true solar time and the 23:00 rule: the same calculation as the BaZi app.
          • Method: the common 紫微斗数全书 rules. The year changes at the lunar new year; a leap-month birth counts as that month to the 15th and the next month from the 16th; 庚 years transform 太阳, 武曲, 太阴, 天同.
          • Checked against the open-source iztro library on nearly 5,000 random charts, with identical results. Star brightness (庙旺利陷) isn't shown yet.
          • This is traditional interpretation, for reflection and fun, not prediction. Charts are kept in this browser only.
